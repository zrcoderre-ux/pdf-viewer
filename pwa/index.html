How case folders open

A whole matter in one pick: the case folder opens as one reader — the Combined Text.txt where the run made one, else the first export in its Text Files folder — with the folder's pseudonym_key.xlsx, its PDFs and its LEAKS.xlsx already attached. Every other export is listed in that reader's Documents panel, a click away, and Find and the leak review read straight through the folder without leaving the tab. A .txt export from PDF-Linker opens in the text reader: real names on screen from the pseudonym key, citations linked, editable. Each PDF opens in its own tab with citation links, comments, OCR and the Table of Authorities.
